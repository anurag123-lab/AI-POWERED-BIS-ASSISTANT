SCHEME DISAMBIGUATION
✅ APPLICABLE SCHEME

Scheme I (ISI Mark Certification)

What You Receive: BIS Licence Certificate & Right to affix Standard Mark (ISI Logo with CM/L Number).

Legal Rationale: Mandated under Household Electrical Appliances QCO 2023 requiring compulsory factory audit inspection and type testing.

🚫 SCHEMES THAT DO NOT APPLY & WHY

Excluded Scheme Exclusions:

Scheme II (CRS): DOES NOT APPLY.
Reason: CRS applies only to designated IT/electronics items under MeitY/MNRE self-declaration. Household appliances mandate full ISI Mark under Scheme-I.
Scheme X: DOES NOT APPLY.
Reason: Scheme X applies to custom heavy industrial equipment built to customer specs.
